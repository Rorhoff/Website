We handle your AppEnhancer Tier 1 support so you don’t have to

AIRevolution is an AI-powered first-line support service: you load your own documentation and screenshots, then paste a customer’s email into the agent. It searches what you’ve provided and drafts a resolution—escalating when the answer isn’t in the knowledge base.

Services & positioning

Audit trail

Tickets in the portal store what the model tried, retrieval hit counts, and final disposition for compliance and coaching.

How it works

  1. Upload AppEnhancer documentation, manuals, and labeled screenshots in Knowledge base.
  2. Set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY on the server for live Claude answers (otherwise you see retrieved context only).
  3. Open Support agent, paste the customer’s message, and optionally log a ticket for tracking.
  4. Review the draft, send it to the customer, or pull it into the Ticket portal for your team.

      Roadmap

      Phases for AIRevolution: marketing site, brain, client portal, diagnostics, and go-to-market—so you and stakeholders share the same picture.

      • Phase 1 — Foundation (weeks 1–2)
        AIRevolution marketing site: hero, services, pricing, how it works, contact/onboarding. Credibility rooted in AppEnhancer expertise. You are here on the product tab.
      • Phase 2 — The brain (weeks 2–4)
        Full knowledge base + agent: all docs, screenshots, guides. Ticket → RAG with Claude, escalation when unknown. (This build includes ingest, retrieval, and Claude wiring for your server key.)
      • Phase 3 — Client portal (weeks 3–5)
        Clients submit issues, see auto-resolved / in review / escalated, and a full AI audit. (Prototype ticket list and audit in Ticket portal.)
      • Phase 4 — Diagnostics (weeks 4–6)
        Scan: user-uploaded screenshots of their instance; vision + cross-check against known issues. Later, lightweight live-page scanning.
      • Phase 5 — Go to market
        Target mid-size AppEnhancer customers; pitch speed and cost vs. traditional support. Pricing band $500–1,500/month by volume.
