Gold featured listings

Gold boosts are delivered through Stripe Checkout. Your card issuer may describe the charge using the merchant name Stripe shows after checkout—this page summarizes how refunds work when listings are removed before Gold time runs out.

What you're paying for

A Gold purchase moves your listing toward the top of results in its category for a set number of days, subject to surge pricing and site rules described at checkout.

If you delete the listing early

No automatic refund. When you voluntarily remove your ad (including from My Ads), unused Gold time is forfeited — the same way you don't get a rebate for cancelling a billboard early after it already ran most of its term.

If we auto-remove your listing before Gold expires

When multiple independent users report your listing and it hits our automated removal threshold, we consider that a marketplace safety action beyond your control. In that scenario we initiate a prorated partial refund through Stripe on the payment that funded your most recent Gold purchase for that listing, based only on unused time left in that purchased window — not earlier stacked renewals paid on the same listing.

Stripe posts the payout per their timing; Stripe's retained processing fees aren't always returned in full, which mirrors how card networks behave industry-wide for partial refunds.

When no refund fires

  • Gold expired before removal (nothing unused).
  • Legacy boosts purchased before our system stored Stripe payment snapshots — we still remove bad listings but can't auto-refund programmatically.
  • Proration rounds below one cent.
  • If Stripe rejects the payout for any technical reason — the listing comes down anyway because it tripped removals; retain your confirmation email so support can escalate with Stripe Dashboard evidence.
