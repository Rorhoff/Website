Gold featured listings

Gold boosts are delivered through Stripe Checkout. Your card issuer may describe the charge using the merchant name Stripe shows after checkout—this page summarizes how refunds work when listings are removed before Gold time runs out.

What you're paying for

A Gold purchase moves your listing toward the top of results in its category for a set number of days, subject to surge pricing and site rules described at checkout.

If your listing is removed before Gold expires

Whether you delete the ad from My Ads or we auto-remove it after repeated reports, we may issue a prorated partial refund through Stripe on your most recent Gold purchase for that listing.

  • You delete: refund is based on days remaining in the active Gold window.
  • We auto-remove (reports): refund is based on days already used — we subtract the value of time you consumed from the refundable pool.

In both cases we first deduct the full original card processing fee (2.9% + $0.30) from what you paid, then prorate the remainder. We do not issue refunds below $1.00. The delete confirmation modal shows the full breakdown before you confirm.

Stripe posts the payout per their timing; processing fees are not returned on partial refunds.

When no refund fires

  • Gold expired before removal (nothing unused).
  • Legacy boosts purchased before our system stored Stripe payment snapshots.
  • Prorated amount is below the $1.00 minimum.
  • Seller delete within 15 minutes of Gold activation, more than five seller refunds in 24 hours, or other anti-abuse limits.
  • Stripe rejects the payout for a technical reason — retain your confirmation email for support.
