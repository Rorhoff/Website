Buyer & Seller Safety

Classifieds connects local buyers and sellers, but every transaction is between the two of you. Please read these tips before meeting up or sending money.

Meet in public places

For in-person handoffs, choose busy, well-lit locations such as a grocery store parking lot, a coffee shop, or your local police station's safe-exchange zone (many U.S. precincts host these). Avoid meeting at private residences with someone you do not know.

Bring someone with you

Tell a friend or family member where you're going, who you're meeting, and when you expect to be back. Bring a buddy along when possible, especially for higher-value items.

Inspect items before paying

Take time to verify the item matches the listing — check serial numbers, confirm electronics power on, and test moving parts. If something feels off or the seller refuses inspection, walk away.

Don't wire money to strangers

  • Avoid bank wire transfers, Western Union, MoneyGram, gift cards, or cryptocurrency for goods you have not yet received.
  • Prefer cash on pickup for local transactions.
  • For shipped items, use a payment platform with buyer protection (e.g. PayPal Goods & Services). "Friends & Family" transfers have no protection.

Never share financial or personal info

  • Don't share your full Social Security number, bank login, credit card number, or one-time passcodes.
  • Legitimate buyers don't need a "verification code" texted to your phone — that's a common takeover scam.
  • Be skeptical of anyone who insists on paying more than the asking price or asks you to forward "extra" funds to a third party.

Watch for common scams

  • Overpayment scams: a "buyer" sends a fake check or payment for more than the price and asks for the difference back.
  • Shipping scams: a "buyer" insists on shipping arrangements you've never heard of and pushes you to send the item before payment clears.
  • Phishing links: never click links in DMs that ask you to "verify your listing" by logging in — navigate to the site directly.
  • Too good to be true: deeply discounted phones, cars, or electronics from sellers who can't meet in person are almost always fake.

Trust your instincts — and report it

If a buyer or seller pressures you, refuses to meet in public, or pushes unusual payment methods, end the conversation. Use the Report this listing button (visible to logged-in users on every ad) to flag suspicious posts — multiple reports trigger automatic removal.

For urgent safety concerns or completed transactions involving theft, contact your local law enforcement.
